# About the Project

## Mission

Mapping the Gay Guides is a digital history project that preserves and analyzes the historical data from the Gay Guides travel series. Our mission is to make this important LGBTQ+ history accessible to researchers, educators, and the general public.

## Background

The Gay Guides were essential resources for queer travelers from the 1960s through the 2000s. During a time when homosexuality was criminalized in many states and discrimination was widespread, these guides provided crucial information about safe spaces, community resources, and LGBTQ+ friendly establishments.

## Our Team

This project is led by researchers and digital humanists committed to preserving LGBTQ+ history. We collaborate with historians, archivists, and community members to ensure the accuracy and cultural sensitivity of our work.

## Data Sources

Our data comes from digitized copies of the Gay Guides series, supplemented by archival research and community contributions. We are committed to transparent data collection and processing methods.
