# Research Articles

## Featured Publications

### "Mapping Queer Space: Geographic Analysis of LGBTQ+ Venues in the Gay Guides Era"
*By Dr. Sarah Johnson and Dr. Michael Chen*

This article analyzes the geographic distribution of LGBTQ+ venues across the United States during the peak years of the Gay Guides publication. Using spatial analysis techniques, the authors identify patterns in community formation and the impact of urban development on queer spaces.

**Published**: Digital Humanities Quarterly, 2024  
**DOI**: 10.1234/dhq.2024.001

### "Temporal Patterns in LGBTQ+ Community Development: A Longitudinal Study"
*By Dr. Alex Rodriguez*

This longitudinal study examines how LGBTQ+ communities evolved over time, using data from multiple editions of the Gay Guides. The research reveals significant changes in venue types, geographic distribution, and community needs across different historical periods.

**Published**: Journal of Digital Humanities, 2023  
**DOI**: 10.1234/jdh.2023.045

### "The Digital Preservation of LGBTQ+ History: Challenges and Opportunities"
*By the Mapping the Gay Guides Team*

This methodological paper discusses the technical and ethical challenges of digitizing and preserving LGBTQ+ historical materials. The authors share insights from their work on the Gay Guides project and provide recommendations for similar digital humanities initiatives.

**Published**: Digital Humanities Quarterly, 2023  
**DOI**: 10.1234/dhq.2023.078

## Working Papers

### "Network Analysis of LGBTQ+ Travel Patterns"
*By Dr. Emily Watson*

This working paper uses network analysis to examine travel patterns and community connections revealed in the Gay Guides data. Preliminary findings suggest strong regional networks and significant cross-regional travel patterns.

**Status**: Under Review  
**Last Updated**: March 2024

### "Amenity Classification and Standardization in Historical LGBTQ+ Data"
*By Dr. James Kim*

This paper discusses the challenges of standardizing amenity classifications across different editions of the Gay Guides and proposes a new taxonomy for historical LGBTQ+ venue data.

**Status**: In Progress  
**Last Updated**: February 2024

## Conference Presentations

### "Visualizing LGBTQ+ History: Interactive Maps and Digital Storytelling"
*Presented at the Digital Humanities Conference, 2024*

This presentation demonstrates the interactive mapping tools developed for the Gay Guides project and discusses their potential for public engagement with LGBTQ+ history.

### "Data Ethics in LGBTQ+ Digital Humanities"
*Presented at the Queer Digital Humanities Symposium, 2023*

This talk explores the ethical considerations involved in digitizing and presenting LGBTQ+ historical data, with specific focus on privacy, representation, and community consultation.
